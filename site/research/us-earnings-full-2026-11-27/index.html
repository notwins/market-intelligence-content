Reuters reported the Bank of England's warning about complex AI financing and stretched valuations; hyperscaler debt issuance is expected to exceed $305bn in 2026. This is a forecast of issuance, not debt outstanding or a US bank earnings result. [S8] On 25 Nov, CoreWeave's 2031 bond yield approached 18%; this is a historical risk-pricing observation, not a default or guaranteed return. [S4]

No individual major NYSE/Nasdaq bank quarterly result with an eligible Reuters EPS and revenue comparison was established. Analysis: test cash interest coverage, refinancing and collateral rather than treating a high yield as safe income.

AI infrastructure and credit transmission

  1. Weaker revenue from AI infrastructure can reduce borrowers’ cash interest coverage.
  2. Higher refinancing costs and falling collateral values can put more pressure on bonds and private loans.
  3. Common exposures can transmit stress to other financial institutions.

9. Three client talking points

1. AI demand is not guaranteed delivery. Track shipment clearance, order conversion and payment receipts instead of assuming that all backlog becomes immediate revenue.

2. Profit matters more than sales alone. Deere's net-sales beat and EPS miss illustrate why a revenue beat is not sufficient; Black Friday promotions require similar margin scrutiny.

3. Financing matters alongside earnings. Stress-test borrowing rates, refinancing and collateral. An elevated bond yield compensates for perceived risk, not guaranteed safety.
