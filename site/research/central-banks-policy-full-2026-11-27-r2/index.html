Transmission & risk map

Fed versus duration

Inflation and growth reports have raised expected policy rates, while risk aversion has supported government bonds. These are not contradictory: front-end rates price the policy path; long yields also reflect growth, term premium and flight-to-quality demand.

USD versus CHF

USD carry is positive for the dollar, but the CHF can strengthen in a shock. For CHF-funded portfolios, the critical risk is the combination of asset prices and FX—not the rate differential alone.

Tariffs

Reuters reported proposed tariffs on 14 partners, a 25 January USTR hearing, and possible implementation no earlier than February/March. Singapore said a proposed 20% rate, if implemented, would raise the effective levy on its exports to the US to 10.3% from 7.8%.
