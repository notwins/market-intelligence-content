4. Consumer / Industrials

Deere's FY27 net income outlook of $4.50bn–$5.25bn trails $5.91bn consensus; it includes a $250m–$400m noncash pension charge. Construction and forestry sales are forecast flat to +10%; data-centre investment supports demand but could slow. [3]

Dollar Tree raised FY26 adjusted EPS guidance to $8.05–$8.25 from $7.70–$8.05. Comparable sales grew 4.6%; tariff refunds helped fund promotions. Walmart's Thanksgiving meal offer is $35, $5 less than last year, despite rising wholesale input costs. [8,9]

Interpretation: holiday traffic may grow while discounts, fuel and other expenses compress margins. Review recurring earnings rather than volume alone.

9. Three client talking points

Revenue growth is not earnings quality. Dell and HPE illustrate different margin and financing exposures. Review cash flow, costs and conversion of orders.

Governance matters to bondholders. Nvidia's divergent rating-agency actions warrant separate credit and equity analysis. Monitor rating changes, spreads and disclosures.

Holiday demand is not automatically profit. Monitor retail volumes, discounts and freight. Stress-test interest rate, FX and leverage risks before increasing exposure.
